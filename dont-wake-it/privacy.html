Summary

Don't Wake It! Monster has no accounts, shows no ads and does not track you. The game never connects to the internet, we do not run servers and we do not collect your data.

Data stored on your device

Your mission progress, stars, best times and scores, collected socks, unlocked skins and themes, daily streak and settings (sound, vibration, screen shake, high-contrast meter) are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. You can erase them with Settings > Reset progress, and deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

This version contains no ads, no analytics or tracking software and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds any of these, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
