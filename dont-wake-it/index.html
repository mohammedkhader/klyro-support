How to play

  • Touch and drag the glowing object, and let go to put it down.
  • Get it into the pulsing zone marked with an arrow.
  • Everything makes noise: speed, sudden jerks, bumps, drops and the surface you drag across. Rugs are quiet, creaky boards are loud.
  • Watch the noise meter. Stop moving and the monster calms down again. If it fills up, the monster wakes and the mission fails.
  • Finish quietly and in time to earn three stars.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no currencies.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline.

How do I unlock the game modes?

Finish Chapter 1 of the story. Endless Night, the Daily Challenge and the Challenge Rooms then appear under Game Modes on the main menu.

How do I turn off sound, vibration or screen shake, or start over?

Open Settings from the main menu. It also has a high-contrast noise meter and a button to reset your progress.
