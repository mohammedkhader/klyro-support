How to play

  • Swipe left or right to change lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide.
  • Collect crystals and power-ups: Spark Magnet pulls in crystals, Thunder Boots give huge jumps, Storm Cloud lets you fly over everything.
  • Hitting an obstacle head-on ends the run. Brushing its side only makes Klyro stumble.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my best score saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes it. On iPhone and iPad, signing in to Game Center also keeps your best score on the Game Center leaderboard.

Why do I see an ad?

Only if you choose it: after a crash you can tap CONTINUE to watch a short video and keep running, once per run. Ads never interrupt a run.

The leaderboard is empty or won't open.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center) and have finished at least one run.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Klyro plays offline. Ads and leaderboards need a connection.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Settings (the gear on the main menu).
