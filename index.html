How to play

  • Touch crystals to charge Klyro's tail. Each of its three glowing cells is one strike.
  • Tap anywhere on the screen to strike. The bolt flies ahead to the nearest target: a storm gate first, otherwise an obstacle or a crystal pylon. If nothing is in reach, the tap does nothing and costs nothing.
  • Storm gates cover the whole track, so strike each one before you reach it. A row of bright storm crystals just before every gate gives you the charge you need.
  • Tap early if you like: when a target is in reach but the tail is empty, your tap waits up to two seconds and fires as soon as Klyro has one strike of charge.
  • Strike a crystal pylon beside the track and the spark leaps along the chain of pylons for bonus points.
  • Swipe left or right to change lane, swipe up to jump and swipe down to slide.
  • Powers use charge. Tail Flare: free strikes for 8 seconds. Fork Bolt: each strike also hits the next two targets for 10 seconds. Crackle Coat: for 12 seconds, a hit becomes a strike instead; this uses one strike of charge, and with an empty tail the coat saves you once and then ends.

The storm map

  • There are 13 storm runs: 7 in Sparkfield Meadow and 6 in Glimmer Cavern. Start at Klyro's Hollow.
  • Finish a storm run to open the next one. Each run has 3 stars: one for reaching the finish and one for each of its two goals.
  • Hit an obstacle head-on, or reach a gate unbroken, and the storm run starts again from the beginning. Tap Retry to go again. There are no continues.
  • The first gate in storm run 1 is a practice gate and cannot end your attempt.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open the Sparkline Loop?

Finish storm run 3, Pylon Ridge. The Sparkline Loop is the bonus endless mode: it keeps going, with storm gates, until a hit ends it.

Where are my stars and best scores saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes them. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Sparkline Loop score also appears on the Game Center leaderboard.

I tapped and nothing happened.

A strike only fires when a target is in reach ahead of Klyro. With an empty tail, the tap waits up to two seconds for charge. A soft fizz means there was nothing to strike, and no charge was spent.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. This version of Klyro has no ads and nothing to buy.

The leaderboard is empty or won't open.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center) and have played the Sparkline Loop at least once.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Klyro plays offline. Game Center leaderboards need a connection.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open the game's Settings (the gear icon).
