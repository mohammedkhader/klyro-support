How to play

  • Towers of 1 to 3 bricks ride the belt to the hammer. Tap anywhere: each tap flattens the top brick.
  • Some towers stand on a spiked bomb. Flatten every brick, then STOP. One tap too many hits the bomb.
  • Leaving a brick standing is a miss. Plain bombs: don't tap at all.
  • Flatten a tower on a bomb and stop in time for a CLEAN STOP bonus. Correct decisions in a row raise your multiplier.
  • You have three lives.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline, including the Daily challenge.

How do I change sound, music, vibration or hints, or start over?

Open Settings from the main menu. It also has a button to reset your progress.
