Privacy Policy

Klyro: Lightning Runner

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026. Developer: Mohammed Khader. Contact: dev.mokh@gmail.com.

Summary

Klyro does not have accounts and we do not run servers that collect your data. Your game progress stays on your device. Optional rewarded video ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, best distance, total crystals, number of runs and settings (music, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores are sent to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards, including for your friends. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

After a crash you may choose to watch a short video ad to keep running. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, Google's software in the app may collect your device's advertising or app identifiers, your approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and diagnostic and performance data. Google may use this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and on iPhone and iPad may use the advertising identifier for tracking only if you allow it in the system prompt. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • You never have to watch an ad to play.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app.

Children

Klyro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
